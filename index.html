How to Use Your Smart Business Card

  • Fill in your details and upload an avatar to create your personalized business card.
  • Upload your resume for quick sharing and download.
  • Download your business card as a JPEG image for sharing or printing.
  • Click Card Details to view, manage, and delete your submitted cards.
  • Use the Job Openings button to explore jobs and check your eligibility instantly.
  • Upload your Smart Card to apply for jobs and download job details as a PDF.

Benefits

  • Instant job eligibility check based on your skills and experience.
  • Quick resume sharing and download links for recruiters.
  • Downloadable job details for your records.
  • Mobile-friendly and easy to update anytime.
